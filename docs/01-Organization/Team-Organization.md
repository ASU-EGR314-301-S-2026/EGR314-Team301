## Team Charter

Our team of ASU student engineers is committed to collaboration, creativity, and the application of technical knowledge. Using GitHub, electrical components, and hands-on system design, we will deliver a functional portfolio-worthy product that reflects our engineering growth. We strive to make an affordable and highly useful product that represents our knowledge and talents.

Our team created this charter to define our shared values and goals for delivering a creative, demonstrable, and semi-functional product.

---

## Product Mission Statement

Our team is committed to making an affordable and accessible exploration product for both residential and commercial use.

---

## Team Structure

Our team is organized around subsystem ownership to ensure clear responsibility and efficient progress throughout the project lifecycle. Each member is responsible for a primary module within the rover system, including sensing, control, communication, power distribution, and integration. This structure allows individual members to focus deeply on their assigned subsystem while still collaborating closely during integration and testing phases. By clearly defining ownership early in the project, we reduce overlap, improve accountability, and simplify troubleshooting when issues arise.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

Each team member maintains ownership of their respective hardware and software components, including schematic design, firmware development, validation testing, and documentation. In addition to subsystem ownership, responsibilities are divided to support project management tasks such as documentation updates, version control oversight, integration scheduling, and presentation preparation. During demonstrations and reviews, each member is prepared to explain both their specific module and how it interfaces with the overall system architecture.

---

## Communication Strategy

The team maintains consistent communication through scheduled meetings and ongoing digital collaboration. Weekly in-person meetings are used to review progress, discuss blockers, and plan integration milestones. Between meetings, coordination is handled through shared repositories and messaging platforms to ensure version control and rapid issue resolution. Technical decisions are discussed collaboratively, with final decisions made based on feasibility, system impact, and alignment with project requirements. This structured communication approach helps prevent integration conflicts and ensures all members remain aligned on system goals.

---

## Development Workflow and Integration

Hardware and software development are performed in parallel within each subsystem before moving into staged integration. Individual modules are first validated independently to confirm functionality and performance against defined requirements. Once validated, modules are integrated incrementally into the full rover architecture. This stepwise integration process reduces system-level risk and allows issues to be isolated efficiently. By validating components before full integration, the team improves overall system reliability and maintains a clear path toward final demonstration readiness.
