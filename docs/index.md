## Team Introduction

Our team is designing and building a modular all terrain rover intended for remote exploration and real time data collection. The goal of this project is to create a system that can operate in environments that may be difficult, unsafe, or inconvenient for direct human access. The rover integrates sensing, wireless communication, control logic, and power management into one cohesive platform. Throughout development, we have focused on building a system that is reliable, organized, and easy to expand as new features are added. By separating major subsystems and clearly defining communication between modules, we are working to ensure that the rover is both functional and maintainable.

---

## Mission Statement

Our mission is to develop a reliable and modular rover platform that supports safe remote exploration while emphasizing structured system design, clear communication between modules, and strong engineering practices.

## Website Overview

This website documents our full engineering process from initial team organization and concept development through requirement definition, system architecture, and protocol design. Each section builds on the previous one to clearly show how early brainstorming evolved into a structured and testable implementation. The organization section introduces team roles and responsibilities, the concept design section explains how ideas were generated and refined, the requirements section defines measurable performance goals, and the block diagram section illustrates how the system is structured to meet those goals. Supporting files, source documents, and supplemental materials are provided in the appendix to ensure transparency and traceability throughout the project.
