## Project Summary
Our team has developed a functional prototype for a modular exploration rover designed for data collection and navigation in an environment. The prototype has mobility, environmental sensing, and imaging by combining two working motors, a humidity sensor, an onboard camera, and Wi-Fi communication. Our engineering process prioritized structured system design and clear communication between modules.

## Team Introduction

Our team is designing and building a modular desert rover intended for remote exploration and real time data collection. The goal of this project is to create a system that can operate in environments that may be difficult, unsafe, or inconvenient for direct human access. The rover integrates sensing, wireless communication, control logic, and power management into one cohesive platform. Throughout development, we have focused on building a system that is reliable, organized, and easy to expand as new features are added. By separating major subsystems and clearly defining communication between modules, we are working to ensure that the rover is both functional and maintainable.

---

## Mission Statement

Our mission is to develop a reliable and modular rover platform that supports safe remote exploration while emphasizing structured system design, clear communication between modules, and strong engineering practices.
